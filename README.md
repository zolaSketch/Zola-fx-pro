# ደጋ / DEGA

Highland aviation intelligence. Built in Ethiopia. Sold wherever the air is thin.

**This is not a weapon.** Dega does not compute targeting, weapons employment, intercept geometry, offensive cyber, or face tracking. It computes density, margin, readiness and the hour you should leave.

## Why this makes money

Western dispatch software assumes the sea. Ethiopia does not have one.

- Bole (HAAB) sits at **2,326 m**
- Debre Tabor (HADT) at **2,588 m**
- Gode (HAGO) at **254 m**

Same country. Two atmospheres. The FAA 120-foot rule of thumb is hundreds of feet wrong on a hot plateau afternoon. That error flips go / reduce / hold — and it burns engines, brakes and payload.

Dega is the first product that treats highland density as a first-class operating system:

1. **Moist-air density engine** — station pressure from QNH, water vapour mixed, ISA density inverted
2. **Diurnal release window** — when this airframe can leave this runway today
3. **Highland cycle debt** — high-DA takeoffs cost more than sea-level cycles
